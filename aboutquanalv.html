Our Origin & Vision

The Problem We Saw

Quanalv was founded in 2024 by Demetrio Alvarez after observing a growing disconnect between major operating system developers and the people who rely on their software every day. Over time, mainstream operating systems had become weighed down by unnecessary background services, confusing menus, and sluggish performance—even on capable hardware.

Worse still, when users ran into issues, getting clear, helpful customer support felt nearly impossible. Demetrio believed computing shouldn't require fighting against your own operating system.

From Project EasyOS to Molcore

During his freshman year, Demetrio set out to build an alternative from the ground up. Initially started under the working name Project EasyOS, the initiative quickly grew into a broader vision for a commercial-grade, closed-source platform: Quanalv Molcore.

The goal was straightforward—strip away unnecessary bloat, keep the interface intuitive enough for any home user, and deliver the security and reliability required by business professionals.

Why FreeBSD?

Building a modern operating system requires solving thousands of low-level hardware challenges before a user ever sees a desktop. After evaluating multiple architectures, Demetrio made the strategic decision to build Molcore on top of FreeBSD.

FreeBSD gave Quanalv a proven, rock-solid core with mature networking, strong security isolation, and broad driver compatibility. Rather than spending years reinventing low-level system plumbing, Quanalv was able to focus its engineering energy on crafting a cohesive, custom user environment and responsive support ecosystem.

What Drives Us

Core Pillars

Uncompromised Speed

Every component in Molcore is engineered to stay lightweight and responsive, ensuring hardware resources go toward your work—not background overhead.

True Simplicity

Powerful software does not need to be complicated. We design clean, predictable interfaces that make sense the first time you sit down at the screen.

Dedicated Support

We treat customer assistance as a core product feature. Both home users and enterprise clients receive direct, knowledgeable support when they need it.
