Organizational Licensing Policy

One License Key for Your Entire Organization

Eliminate per-seat activation audits, seat-count renewals, and per-machine key tracking across your internal workstations.

When your organization purchases Molcore Business Edition, Quanalv Corporation issues an official Organizational License Key registered directly to your company, school, agency, or commercial entity.

Unlike standard consumer licenses tied to a single workstation, your Business Edition license key can register and activate as many computers as needed, with no artificial seat caps or per-device activation fees—provided every activated machine belongs to and operates within your organization.

Official Business Licensing Note

Purchasing Molcore for Business (Molcore Business Edition) includes a commercial license key that can register as many computers as needed as long as they are within the organization. Redistribution, resale, or activation on systems outside the licensed organizational entity is strictly prohibited.
