HOUSEHOLD LICENSING POLICY

One Perpetual Pack for Up to Five Home Computers

Eliminate individual operating system purchases, per-PC office subscriptions, and mandatory cloud account sign-ins across your family’s desktop and laptop systems.

When your household purchases Quanalv Molcore Family Pack 1.0.0, Quanalv Corporation issues a unified 5-Seat Household Master Key alongside physical or downloadable ISO installation media for both the operating system and productivity suite.

Unlike single-seat retail licenses restricted to one motherboard, your Family Pack key authorizes up to five separate personal computers located within the same household. Each machine activates independently via the Molcore Activation Portal or offline certificate exchange without tying family members to centralized cloud telemetry.

All five installations receive perpetual ownership of Quanalv Molcore 1.0 and Quanalv Office 1.0, complete with local-first security updates, hardware driver support, and offline media reinstallation rights.
