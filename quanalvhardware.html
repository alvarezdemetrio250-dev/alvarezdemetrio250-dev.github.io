SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS

Baseline & Workstation Requirements

Quanalv Molcore 1.0 is engineered with a zero-bloat footprint to run smoothly on both modern enterprise workstations and older x86_64 desktops.

PROCESSOR & FIRMWARE

64-Bit x86_64 Architecture

Minimum: Dual-core 1.6 GHz x86_64 (Intel Core 4th Gen+ or AMD Ryzen/FX).
Firmware: UEFI 2.3+ (Secure Boot optional) or Legacy BIOS.

MEMORY & STORAGE

Low-Overhead Footprint

Memory: 4 GB RAM minimum (8 GB+ recommended for Quanalv Grid & Office).
Storage: 24 GB free space on NVMe, SATA SSD, or SAS drive.

GRAPHICS & NETWORKING

Native Kernel Drivers

Graphics: Intel UHD/Iris Xe, AMD Radeon GCN+, or UEFI GOP framebuffer.
Network: Intel Gigabit/2.5GbE, Realtek RTL8111/8125, Intel Wi-Fi 6.
