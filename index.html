Our Story

How It All Began

Quanalv became an idea in 2024 out of a simple observation by founder Demetrio Alvarez: modern operating systems had lost sight of the user. Too often, major OS providers deliver bloated software that slows down hardware, relies on overly complex interfaces, and treats customer support as an afterthought.

Determined to change this, Demetrio began developing a better alternative during his freshman year in high school. Initially known as Project EasyOS, the vision quickly evolved into Quanalv Molcore—an operating system designed from the ground up for speed, simplicity, and dedicated user support.

Finding the right foundation for Molcore was a critical challenge. After exploring several options, Demetrio chose to build the operating system on top of FreeBSD. This strategic decision provided the robust hardware compatibility, driver support, and stability of an established platform, freeing Quanalv to innovate and craft a completely custom user experience without spending years reinventing the core infrastructure.

Today, Quanalv remains committed to that original mission: building fast, intuitive software that puts people first.
