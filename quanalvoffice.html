LOCAL-FIRST ARCHITECTURE

Full Desktop Ownership Without Recurring Subscriptions

Eliminate annual per-user seat subscriptions, forced cloud storage sync, and background AI data harvesting across your organization.

Quanalv Office 1.0 is compiled natively in C++ for deterministic speed on Quanalv Molcore 1.0. Cold startup takes under 350 milliseconds, and multi-hundred-megabyte spreadsheets calculate locally without browser rendering overhead or network latency.

Every license is perpetual. Once activated online or via offline hardware certificate on quanalvofficeactivation.html, your installation remains permanently unlocked on your workstation or across your entire organization under a Business Volume Key.

Full read, write, and macro-safe translation for .DOCX, .XLSX, .PPTX, .ODF, and PDF/A ensures seamless document exchange with external partners using Microsoft Office or LibreOffice.

Quanalv Write

Precision typography, legal redlining, master document indexing, and deterministic pagination for technical manuals, contracts, and long-form reports.

SPREADSHEETS & MODELING

Quanalv Grid

Multi-threaded calculation engine supporting 16 million rows, 480+ financial and statistical functions, pivot tables, and deterministic formula auditing.

Formats: .QGRD, .XLSX, .XLS, .CSV, .ODS
PRESENTATIONS

Quanalv Deck

Hardware-accelerated slide composition, vector diagramming, dual-monitor presenter console, and zero-dependency self-contained slide playback.

Formats: .QDCK, .PPTX, .PPT, .ODP, PDF
RELATIONAL DESKTOP DATABASE

Quanalv Base

Embedded SQLite and PostgreSQL client front-end for building internal inventory forms, query designers, and automated printable operational reports.

Formats: .QDB, SQLite, PostgreSQL, ODBC
EMAIL & SCHEDULING

Quanalv Mail

Local IMAP, POP3, CalDAV, and CardDAV desktop client with built-in PGP/S-MIME encryption, offline mail indexing, and zero third-party cloud relays.

Protocols: IMAP4, SMTP, CalDAV, S/MIME
